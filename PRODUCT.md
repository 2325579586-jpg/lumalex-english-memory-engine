# Product

## Register

product

## Users

LumaLex serves English learners, especially university students, exam-prep learners, and self-directed readers who study vocabulary on mobile and desktop. They use the app in short focused sessions to add words, learn new sets, review due items, practice spelling, and keep progress synced across devices.

## Product Purpose

LumaLex is a memory-first English vocabulary system. It turns words, phrases, examples, pronunciation, relations, and review history into a daily recall workflow, so learners can move from first exposure to durable memory instead of maintaining a passive word list.

## Brand Personality

Calm, focused, encouraging. The interface should feel like a quiet study companion: premium enough to invite daily use, restrained enough to stay out of the learner's way, and supportive without becoming game-heavy.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy gamified dashboards, dense admin-table layouts, marketing-style landing-page composition inside the app, generic SaaS chrome, and overly decorative UI that distracts from recall. Avoid hiding the answer too late or revealing it too early in study flows.

## Design Principles

1. Keep recall first: every study surface should make the learner answer before revealing help.
2. Make progress legible: show where the learner is, what changed, and what happens next.
3. Keep mobile actions thumb-ready: primary study decisions should stay easy to reach.
4. Treat relations as learning material: examples, synonyms, antonyms, and derived words should be actionable, not just decorative metadata.
5. Preserve calm density: enough information for serious study, arranged so the current task remains obvious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Favor readable contrast, large touch targets, keyboard-friendly controls, clear disabled and loading states, and reduced-motion-safe transitions. Chinese and English text can appear together, so layouts must handle mixed scripts, long words, and compact mobile widths without overflow.
